Always-on-top desktop timer, Pomodoro, Flowtime & focus analytics.

While the browser extension stays inside your web tabs, Jelly Timer Plus (PRO) runs natively above all Windows and macOS applications — IDEs, Figma, Notion, spreadsheets, and documents.

Pomodoro & Flowtime

Automated work/break intervals and uninterrupted Flowtime mode that calculates a smart 1/5 break duration based on your actual focus time.

Auto-Focus by Active Window

Automatically resumes your timer when you switch into work applications (VS Code, Word, Figma, Docs) and pauses when you switch away.

7-Day Focus Analytics

Visual 7-day bar chart of deep focus minutes, completed session counter, and automatic log of top applications used during focus.

528 Hz Zen & Chime Audio

Choose between classic alarm, soft harmonic Chime chord, and 528 Hz Zen tone, plus Deep Obsidian and Slate Titanium themes.
